A state model that keeps every outcome explainable

State transitions must record actor, time, reason, and prior state. Policy-dependent transitions require configuration and authorization.

Answers: “Where can an application go?”

Primary route

Draft→Submitted→Under review→Assessment→Offered→Accepted→Enrolled

Rework and capacity

Needs information↔Under reviewWaitlisted→ seat available →Offered

Closed outcomes

RejectedWithdrawnExpiredCancelled

Data boundaries and integration responsibilities

Sensitive student/support information receives stricter visibility. External callbacks are verified, idempotent, observable, and safe to retry.
